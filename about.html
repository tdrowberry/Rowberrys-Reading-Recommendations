About Tyler

Why this whole list exists.

Over the past two and a half years I've read somewhere around 300 books — fantasy, sci-fi, history, parenting, business, and everything in between. A big part of why I kept reading at that pace was this exact list: I wanted a real, honest set of recommendations I could hand to friends and family, read aloud to my own kids now, and eventually let them use to pick good books for themselves once they're old enough to choose on their own.

That's also why a couple of two-star books are still sitting on these shelves. This isn't a highlight reel of only the best — it's my actual reading record, so the five-star picks mean something precisely because the low ones weren't quietly removed.

I'm also working toward writing a fiction book of my own someday. If you've got thoughts on storytelling, publishing, or just want to talk about books, I'd genuinely love to hear them.

Connect and contact me on LinkedIn — that's the best place to reach me.
